Confidential working paper · September 2026

Australia launch plan.

How actr.one becomes the career desk Australian actors actually keep open — starting in Perth, then the rest of the country — without pretending to be a casting marketplace.

Prepared for an Australian-first commercial launch of actr.one (Call Sheet / Studio / Pro). Figures in AUD, GST-inclusive unless noted. Sources cited in §12.

1. Executive summary

Australia is in a production boom and an actor-admin mess. Drama spend hit a record $2.7 billion in FY2024/25 (Screen Australia). Perth Film Studios is open, with the first lot production already shooting. Actors still run their careers across Casting Networks, StarNow, email, Notes, a spreadsheet, and a coach they cannot afford every week.

actr.one is not another submissions portal. It is the operating system between submissions: sides, tapes, callbacks, notes, training, and a board-aware coach. Call Sheet is free. Studio and Pro are the paid desks.

The launch is Perth-first because the founder is here, WAAPA and the Perth Actors Centre are concentrated, and the new Malaga lot is putting WA actors into more rooms this year. Sydney and Melbourne are year-two density plays, not year-one vanity markets.

  • Do not compete with Casting Networks or Showcast. Sit beside them. Import nothing until we have permission and a reason.
  • Price in Australian dollars, GST-inclusive, cancel anytime. Studio A$19/mo or A$190/yr. Pro A$39/mo or A$390/yr.
  • Sell the work, not the dream. Copy stays actor-native. No “10x your bookings”.
  • Bootstrap to $75k GST threshold, then decide on a raise. This is a craft tool with a small, high-trust market — not a venture-scale marketplace.

What we are not

  • Not a casting directory, availability calendar for CDs, or submission button.
  • Not an agent CRM. Agents get a later “export the book” PDF, not another login.
  • Not a social network. No follower counts. The run is craft, not clout.

3. The Australian market

Demand-side facts

  • $2.7bn drama expenditure in Australia, FY2024/25, 174 titles — up 43% year on year (Screen Australia Drama Report).
  • $1.1bn of that on 71 Australian titles; international features and SVOD drove the rest, including a record ~$1.0bn on 20 international feature shoots.
  • Jobs and Skills Australia (ANZSCO 2111, 2025): 8,400 employed as actors, dancers and other entertainers. 59% part-time. Median age 33. Median full-time weekly earnings $1,692 — which hides the much larger unpaid / mixed-income population.
  • State mix of that employed group: NSW 31%, VIC 30%, QLD 19%, WA 10%, SA 6%, others ~4%.
  • MEAA Equity, 30 Jun 2025: 5,488 financial members (5,176 full + 312 students). This is the professional core, not the ceiling.

Why Australia, why now

Production is up, titles are lumpy

Fewer Australian titles (89 → 71) but more spend per title and a flood of incoming shoots. Actors feast/famine harder. A desk that survives the quiet week is the product.

Perth is no longer a flyover

Perth Film Studios (Malaga) is operating. Two Birds (Stan/ITV) is the first lot shoot — ~$17m into WA, 100+ local cast and crew. Screenwest incentives are pulling work west. Local actors need the same OS as the east, without waiting for Sydney tools to care.

5. Beachhead strategy

Do not spray Sydney Facebook groups in month one. Density beats reach. A hundred Perth actors who actually log tapes will sell Melbourne faster than a thousand unused accounts.

Phase A — Perth / Boorloo (months 0–6)

  • WAAPA. Acting (~18) and music theatre (~20) graduating classes plus Open / short-course students. Offer a graduate pack: Call Sheet + one Studio month at graduation showcase.
  • Perth Actors Centre and PACAS. Teacher-led: a 10-minute “put tonight’s scene on the board” at the end of class. Teachers get a free Pro seat if they put a cohort on Call Sheet.
  • Self-tape rooms and readers. A printed call-sheet pad and a QR on the light stand: “Log the take before you pack the C-stand.”
  • Screenwest / lot adjacency. Not a funding application on day one. Show up at open days, attachments, and WA-cast productions with a tool that helps local principals keep three holds honest.
  • Founder-led. One night a week in a class or a green room. No ads.

Phase B — Second cities (months 4–10)

Brisbane and Adelaide before Sydney. Same shape as Perth: one school, one independent studio, one self-tape room. QUT, USQ, Flinders, Adelaide College of the Arts. Casting is thinner; the spreadsheet pain is worse.

Phase C — Density markets (months 8–18)

Melbourne (theatre + TVC + comedy) and Sydney (screen + NIDA / ACA / Screenwise / The Studio by Casting Networks). Enter with proof: “WAAPA year used this for showcase season” — not with a brand campaign.

6. Go-to-market

Principles

  1. Actors trust actors. Every channel is a person with a board, not a media buy.
  2. Teachers and readers are the distribution. Pay them in product and respect, not affiliate spam.
  3. MEAA is a community to serve, not a list to harvest. Equity Foundation PD, not cold DMs to the membership roll.
  4. First Nations, regional, and disability-led performers are in the beachhead on purpose — WA work is often regional; the desk has to travel.

Always-on channels (cheap, on-voice)

  • X / Instagram. Sides energy, not growth-hack carousels. One craft post, one product post, per week. Link in bio to Call Sheet.
  • Email. Transactional + a monthly “call sheet” from us: three industry notes, one product note. Unsubscribe honoured like a human.
  • Schools. End-of-year showcase packs. A printed card in the program: “Keep the roles in one place.”
  • Referral. Later: “Bring your scene partner” — one extra Studio month each. Not in month one.

What we will not do

  • Buy Casting Networks keywords to impersonate a job board.
  • Spam MEAA Facebook groups.
  • Pay for fake testimonials.
  • Discount Pro to $1 to game conversion. Limits and price are the product honesty.

10. Company, legal, data

  • Entity. WA-registered Pty Ltd. Founder in Perth. ASIC, registered office, $1–$10 shares until a raise exists.
  • Trading name. actr.one. Domain already is the brand. ABN on invoices.
  • Payments. Stripe Australia, AUD. Apple/Google in-app not required (web app). Keep plans cancelable in Settings.
  • Consumer law. Subscriptions: clear price, GST, renew date, one-click cancel. No dark patterns. ACL 14-day cooling-off for unsolicited sales; we are not door-to-door, but we still honour a short refund window.
  • Privacy. APPs. Board data is personal (name, roles, notes). Publish a short Privacy Policy: what we store, that craft events live with the board, that coach prompts send board context to a model, that we do not sell data. Host region: prefer AU or be honest if not. Do not train public models on notes.
  • AI disclosure. The coach is a model. Say so. Not a CD, not a psychologist, not MEAA advice.
  • Not a casting service. Terms: we do not offer work, take commission, or contact CDs on your behalf. That keeps us out of the Casting Networks / agent fight.
  • IP. Brand lockup, product, copy. Actors keep their notes and sides. We do not claim their material.
  • Insurance. Cyber + professional indemnity once we take paid Pro (coach advice-adjacent). Public liability if we run in-person classes.

11. Product work that the launch needs

The desk is already a beta OS. Australia go-live is not a rewrite. It is a short commercial and localisation pass.

  1. AUD prices, GST label, Stripe, invoices.
  2. Privacy Policy, Terms, AI disclosure, refund copy.
  3. Timezone and date: Australia/Perth default for WA accounts; let them set Sydney/Melbourne. Call times as local.
  4. Copy pass: “self-tape”, “CD”, “MEAA”, “hold”, “avail” — already close. Add AU examples in empty states, not US network slang.
  5. Export: one PDF “book” page (roles + status). Agents asked for this in every adjacent tool.
  6. School code: a teacher can mint 30 Call Sheet seats for a term.
  7. Uptime and backup for account/plan DB. Board is on-device today — document that clearly so a lost phone is a known risk, or add optional encrypted export before we take money.

Blocker: do not run paid ads or charge Perth actors while board data is device-local without an export. A lost phone during showcase week would end the brand. Ship export (and ideally cloud backup for paid) before Stripe goes live.

13. Team and capital

Y1 shape: one founder-operator (product + Perth rooms) + a fractional accountant + a privacy/terms lawyer (fixed job) + design only if the brand lockup needs print.

Capital: bootstrap. A $40–80k friends-and-family round only if we need cloud backup + 6 months of founder wage. Not before 50 paying desks. No agency retainers.

If it works by month 18: one community hire in Melbourne or Sydney (actor who already uses the desk), still no ads team.

14. Decision checklist

  • ☐ Pty Ltd + ABN + Stripe AU
  • ☐ Privacy, Terms, AI disclosure
  • ☐ Board export (and paid backup)
  • ☐ AUD / GST prices in the product
  • ☐ 10 Perth design partners on Pro comp
  • ☐ One school + one studio + one tape-room QR
  • ☐ Charge the first non-comp Studio from a real limit, not a discount code

When those seven are ticked, actr.one is an Australian company selling to Australian actors. Until then it is a strong beta. Do not confuse the two.
