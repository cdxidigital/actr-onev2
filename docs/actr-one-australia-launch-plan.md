# actr.one — Australian Launch Business Plan

**Confidential · operating plan, not a pitch deck**
**Prepared:** September 2026
**Horizon:** 18 months from incorporation
**Home market:** Australia, with Perth as the first city
**Entity assumption:** Australian Pty Ltd trading as actr.one (under CDXI Digital or a new vehicle)

---

## 1. Executive summary

actr.one is an actor operating system: one desk for live roles, self-tapes, character notes, training, career health, and a coach that has actually read the board. Call Sheet is free. Studio is the working-actor plan. Pro is the busy desk.

Australia is the right first country.

- The product already speaks the local craft (sides, tapes, callbacks, holds).
- Casting infrastructure here is fragmented: Showcast is the directory, Casting Networks and StarNow compete at the edges, and the *week itself* still lives in Notes, email, and a camera roll named `FINAL_v7`.
- Actors already pay for the industry door. Showcast is about A$180/year. Casting Networks Premium is A$20.99/month. actr.one sits *after* the breakdown lands — complementary, not a replacement.
- Founder is in Perth. WAAPA is a national feeder with a tight network. A small city that takes the product seriously beats a Sydney launch that nobody feels.

This is a bootstrap plan, not a venture story. Year-one success is a few hundred Australian desks that stay open, a paying core that covers AI and ops, and a reputation inside three drama schools and two self-tape rooms. Year-two success is a default tool in Melbourne and Sydney working weeks.

## 2. The product as it ships

What exists today, and what a paying Australian actor is buying.

| Layer | What it is | Why it matters in AU |
|---|---|---|
| Call Sheet (free) | 8 live roles, 6 notes, 3 tape checklists, 3 coach asks/month, two looks, the run | Habit before money. Matches drama-school and first-tape years. |
| Studio (paid) | Unlimited board, five looks, modular dashboard, analytics, 20 coach asks | The working week: three jobs, one instrument. |
| Pro (paid) | 80 deeper coach reads | Recurring, holds, full book. |
| The run | Ranks, daily sides, marks earned from logged work | Habit loop without turning craft into a game. |
| Coach | Reads the actual board. Quota is the real cost of goods. | The only line that scales with usage. |

Beta today: plans activate instantly and are not charged. Australian launch turns that into real AUD billing with GST, a privacy notice that satisfies the APPs, and copy that names the local stack (Showcast, MEAA, AEST).

**Not in v1, on purpose:** marketplace, agent portal, Showcast sync, job board. Those are later wedges. Launch sells the desk.

## 3. Market

### 3.1 How big is “actor” in Australia

Official numbers undercount the people who audition.

| Source | Figure | How to read it |
|---|---|---|
| Jobs and Skills Australia, ANZSCO 2111 (actors, dancers and other entertainers) | **8,400 employed** | Employed only. 59% part-time. Median age 33. NSW 31%, VIC 30%, QLD 19%, WA 10%. |
| MEAA | Equity **~5,500 financial members**; broader Equity footprint larger | The professional floor. Residuals, awards, the people who already treat this as a job. |
| Drama schools | NIDA, VCA, WAAPA (~18 acting places/year at WAAPA from 700+ applicants), QUT, plus NIDA Open / TAFE / private | Annual emerging cohort in the low hundreds nationally; alumni network in the thousands. |
| Casting platforms | Showcast is the professional default; StarNow is the wide net; Casting Networks AU at A$209.90/year | Anyone with an active Showcast profile is in the SAM. |

**TAM (Australia, people who might keep a career desk):** ~25,000–40,000. Includes working actors, emerging, returning, extras who self-tape, serious students.

**SAM (people who self-tape or hold a live pipeline in a given year):** ~12,000–20,000.

**SOM year 1 (what we can actually reach by hand):** ~2,000 conversations. Conversion target: 800 accounts, 140 paying.

Screen Australia’s 2024/25 Drama Report: **A$2.7 billion** drama expenditure, 174 titles, international TV/VOD spend up more than 4×. More tapes, more holds, more weeks that need a desk — not more directories.

| City | Share of 2111 employment | Role in this plan |

Perth first is a feature. WAAPA acting takes ~18 a year and is watched nationally. A product that the 2026 and 2027 companies actually use will travel east with them.

## 4. Customer

### 4.1 Beachhead ICP (first 200 paying)

Working or emerging screen/theatre actor, 20–38, in Perth or Melbourne, who:

1. has a Showcast (or is about to)
2. self-tapes at least twice a month
3. already pays for *something* professional (headshots, class, Showcast)
4. is tired of rebuilding the week in Notes

Not the ICP: people hunting a “get me an agent” app. Not extras-only volume. Not US voice from day one.

### 4.2 Segments and plan fit

| Segment | Plan | Trigger |
|---|---|---|
| Drama school / first tapes | Call Sheet | Class sides, first self-tape, free habit |
| Working week (3 live jobs) | Studio | Hits the 8-role cap, wants analytics + 20 asks |
| Recurring / busy book | Pro | Coach becomes weekly rehearsal partner |
| Teacher / CD (later) | Studio seat, then school licence | “My students keep missing due times” |

### 4.3 Jobs to be done

- Know what is actually live tonight.
- Finish the tape without a missing slate.
- Keep the instrument warm when the board is full.
- Show an agent or yourself that the week had a shape.

## 5. Pricing (Australia)

Charge in **AUD, GST-inclusive**. Publish the GST-exclusive figure on invoices. Do not leave the current US-looking $14 / $28 on an Australian card.

| Plan | Monthly (inc GST) | Yearly (inc GST) | Logic |
|---|---|---|---|
| Call Sheet | A$0 | A$0 | Habit. 8 live roles is a real week for emerging; the cap is the upgrade. |
| Studio | **A$19** | **A$190** (2 months free) | Under Casting Networks monthly. ~A$1.46/week. Less than one hour of studio hire. |
| Pro | **A$35** | **A$350** | For the desk that already asks the coach every week. |

GST exclusive: Studio A$17.27, Pro A$31.82.

**Why not $14 AUD:** too cheap to feel like a professional tool, and it trains the market that the coach is free. **Why not $29 Studio:** that is Casting Networks territory and we are not a submission platform.

Annual is the cash-flow preference (Stripe + one reminder). Offer monthly because actors’ income is lumpy.

**Tax deduction:** professional actors can generally claim software used to produce income. State this as a prompt to speak to their accountant, never as advice.

**Founding 100:** first 100 Studio annuals at A$140/year (inc GST), locked for 24 months, named. Creates cash, testimonials, and a Perth/Melbourne cohort.

## 7. Go-to-market

### 7.1 Principle

Actors do not adopt tools from ads. They adopt tools from the person who sat next to them in class, the self-tape operator who said “use this checklist”, and the graduate who still answers their phone.

Spend almost nothing on paid social until 200 weekly-active desks exist. Spend the founder’s time in rooms.

### 7.2 Beachhead sequence

**Phase A — Perth, months 0–3 (private beta → city beta)**

- 25 known actors (WAAPA alumni, local agents’ lists, State Theatre extra lists). Concierge onboarding. Weekly office hours at a cafe or hire-studio.
- 3 self-tape rooms (Perth CBD / Fremantle / WAAPA-adjacent): printed card on the desk, 10% off Studio for anyone who books that room this month.
- 1 scene-study teacher: class set of Call Sheet, teacher on Studio.
- Language: “Built in Perth. Used on a working week.”

**Phase B — Melbourne, months 4–8**

- VCA + independent scene-study (Howard, 16th St, etc. — pick two that will actually champion).
- Theatre dense: MTC/STC-adjacent networks via people, not partnerships.
- One Melbourne self-tape studio as the eastern reference customer.

**Phase C — Sydney, months 7–14**

- Arrive with 40+ eastern-states testimonials, not a cold homepage.
- Agent-adjacent: never sell “we replace your agent”. Sell “your client arrives prepared”.
- NIDA Open / short-course teachers before NIDA degree politics.

**Phase D — Schools pack, months 6–18**

- “Company licence”: 20 Call Sheet seats + 1 Studio teacher, A$0 in year one if they give a written case study; A$490/year after.
- Target: WAAPA acting + one Melbourne school + one Sydney short-course.

### 7.3 Channels that work here

| Channel | Why | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Drama school companies | Dense, trusted, annual refresh | End-of-year showcase season (Sep–Nov): *do not* pitch agents; pitch the graduates leaving for Melbourne/Sydney. |
| Self-tape studios | Point of pain | Physical card. Operator affiliate: A$30 credit per Studio annual they send. |
| MEAA-adjacent rooms | Professional identity | Speak at a branch social if invited. Never scrape the member list. |
| Actor Facebook groups (closed) | Where the week is actually discussed | Founder posts as an actor with a desk, not a brand. One post per group per month, useful, no funnel. |
| Scene partners | Viral loop that isn’t fake | “Bring your reader” — second Call Sheet from a shared tape. |
| Instagram / TikTok | Proof of desk, not ads | 20-second “three live jobs, one board”. Face + product. No SaaS stock. |
| Email | Retention | Sunday 6pm AEST/AWST: “three things on the board”. Only if they opted in. |

### 7.4 What we will not do

- Pay for Showcast ads or CD dinners.
- Spam agents.
- Run “book more roles” claims. ACL (Australian Consumer Law) will eat that alive, and it is a lie.
- Discount Call Sheet. It is already free.

### 7.5 Launch narrative

> actr.one is the desk Australian actors were already improvising in Notes. Built here. Call Sheet is free. Studio is A$19/month. Showcast still gets you seen.

## 8. Unit economics

All figures AUD, GST-inclusive revenue unless noted. COGS estimated.

| | Studio monthly | Pro monthly |
|---|---:|---:|
| Price inc GST | 19.00 | 35.00 |
| GST (1/11) | 1.73 | 3.18 |
| Net of GST | 17.27 | 31.82 |
| Stripe (≈1.7% + A$0.30) | 0.63 | 0.90 |
| Coach COGS (blended; most users use < half quota) | 1.50 | 4.00 |
| **Contribution / month** | **~15.10** | **~26.90** |
| Contribution margin (net of GST) | ~87% | ~85% |

Annual plans improve cash and cut Stripe events; COGS still tracks usage.

**Blended notes**

- Coach is the only expensive feature. Cap and metering already exist. Do not “unlimited coach” to win a deal.
- Free users cost almost nothing except the 3 asks/month. Watch abuse; already quota’d.
- Target: CAC under A$25 (mostly time). Payback on Studio monthly < 2 months of contribution.
- Churn assumption year 1: 8% monthly on monthly plans, 20% annual at renewal. Actors vanish between jobs. Design pause, not punishment (keep the board on Call Sheet).

**Capacity:** founder-led support until 500 accounts. After that, one part-time community person (see costs).

## 10. Legal, entity, compliance

Not legal advice. This is the work list for an Australian solicitor and accountant.

| Item | Why | When |
|---|---|---|
| ABN | Invoices, GST, Stripe AU | Week 1 |
| Pty Ltd (or sole trader → Pty Ltd at first dollar of risk) | Limits personal exposure; looks like a company agents can invoice) | Week 1–2 |
| Business name actr.one | Trading name | Week 1 |
| GST | See above | At first paid plan |
| Privacy policy aligned to **Australian Privacy Principles** | Even below the $3m Privacy Act threshold, APP-style policy is the professional bar; AI coach processes career data | Before first paid AU user |
| Customer terms + ACL-safe refunds | Digital product, 7-day cooling-off style refund on first month is cheap insurance | Before billing |
| Consumer guarantees | Don’t claim bookings, rankings that “get you seen”, or guaranteed callbacks | Copy review |
| Copyright in user content | Actor owns sides/notes; licence to process for the coach | Terms |
| AI disclosure | Coach is a model. Not a substitute for agent, solicitor, or MEAA. | In-product + terms |
| Notifiable data breaches scheme | If you hold personal information and become an APP entity | Process on paper now |
| Workers comp / super | Only if hiring | Year 2 |
| Trademark | Cheap vs a later fight | Month 2 |

**Data residency:** board is currently device-local + account on server (plan/quota). For AU launch, say that clearly. Do not pretend data lives in Sydney if it does not. If you later store boards in the cloud, prefer an AU or at least documented region and say so.

**Payments:** Stripe Australia, AUD, Apple Pay / Google Pay, cards. BECS Direct Debit later for annual (1% capped A$3.50) once volume exists.

## 16. Why Australia, why now

1. **The work is here.** A$2.7bn drama year, more international shooting, more self-tapes per actor per month than the industry had language for.
2. **The hole is here.** Directories are paid and mature. The week is still amateur.
3. **The price is here.** A$19 sits under CN and beside a coffee-and-tram week, and is deductible for many.
4. **The founder is here.** Perth is a small enough room to be known and a serious enough school to export the habit.
5. **The product is here.** Beta is empty-desk honest. Charging Australians for a sample board full of “The Last Signal” would have been a lie. That is already gone.
